# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the Clack community welcoming and
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that support an open, respectful, healthy, and
productive community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that strengthens the community include:

- showing empathy and respect for different perspectives and experiences;
- giving and receiving technical feedback constructively;
- focusing criticism on ideas and code rather than people;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and
  learning from the experience;
- respecting privacy and avoiding disclosure of another person's private data;
- prioritizing the project's and community's long-term health.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome sexual attention, or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- deliberate intimidation, stalking, sustained disruption, or incitement of
  harassment;
- abuse of project reporting, moderation, or contribution processes;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
reviews, discussions, commits, community events, and private project
communications. It also applies when an individual officially represents Clack
in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers using the repository's
available private contact or moderation channel. If no private channel is shown,
open a public issue asking for a private point of contact without including
sensitive details.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Maintainers
will consider the context, impact, and wishes of the person affected. Retaliation
against someone who makes a good-faith report or participates in its review is
itself a violation.

Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the
conduct-reporting process.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of
acceptable behavior. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments,
commits, code, issues, and other participation that conflicts with this Code.

Depending on impact, intent, and pattern of behavior, consequences may include:

1. **Correction:** a private explanation and request to change behavior.
2. **Warning:** a formal warning with clear consequences for recurrence.
3. **Temporary restriction:** a time-limited ban from interaction or public
   communication in project spaces.
4. **Permanent restriction:** removal from project participation for sustained,
   severe, or repeated violations.

Maintainers who have a conflict of interest should recuse themselves from the
report and review. Enforcement decisions should protect the safety and dignity
of the community while remaining proportionate and consistent.
